# AI Opportunity Audit

**Find out where AI actually pays off in your business, before you build or buy anything.**

**What it does:** Claude interviews you, one question at a time, across four areas: your business, your week, where things leak, and where the money comes from. Then it writes a ranked report. Every idea has to pass two gates: can AI genuinely do this today, and will it drive results for *your* business. Ideas that fail either are listed as excluded, with a reason. The rest are ranked by estimated hours saved per week, and the top three each get one concrete first step.

If the top pick is an AI that acts (sends emails, edits records), a short safety checklist is included: an owner, a "never does this" list, a rollback plan.

**Before:** you know you "should be using AI", you try a few tools, and half-finished experiments pile up.
**After:** one ranked list, three first steps, and a clear no on the ideas that wouldn't pay.

Hours saved are estimates from your own account of your week, labeled as such. The report ends with a next step: take #1 into **structure-first-ai** (also in this repo) to design the build.

**How to use it:** say "run the AI opportunity audit" and answer the questions. Loose answers are fine; it digs where it needs more.
