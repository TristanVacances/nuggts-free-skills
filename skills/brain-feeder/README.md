# Brain Feeder

**Everything you save (reels, posts, videos, articles) becomes skills and notes your AI actually uses, instead of dying in your Saved folder.**

**What it does:** you give it a batch of what you saved: pasted links with captions or transcripts, or the saved-posts export a platform lets you download. It:
1. sorts each item into new skill, upgrade to a skill you already have, note for your vault, or discard
2. stages the results without touching your live setup
3. gives you one review digest, with unverified claims and gaps flagged
4. promotes only the items you approve

Run it whenever you have saved a batch. There is no schedule and no browser automation; you stay in control of your accounts.

**Before:** hundreds of saved posts you never reopen, and a vague memory that one of them had the answer.
**After:** the useful ones are skills your AI follows, or notes it can find, with the creator credited. The rest is discarded on purpose.

Works with the optional sibling skill `skill-integrator` for overlap checks and clean merges; it has a built-in fallback if that skill is not installed.

**Credits:** a Nuggts original. Respect platform terms and creators' rights: skills are written in your own words and credit the creator.
