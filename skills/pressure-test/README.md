# Pressure-Test

**Find out if your idea is any good before you spend a year on it.** Your idea gets attacked from several independent angles, and you get a straight verdict plus the better version if there is one.

**What it does:** describe your business idea, side project or plan. It:
1. names the real risk (demand, distribution, money, copycats, or fit with you)
2. gathers the evidence it can actually check, and labels anything it couldn't verify
3. runs separate passes as a customer, a skeptic, an economist and a contrarian, each writing before seeing the others' verdicts
4. leads with the call: go, skip, or needs homework
5. gives a rescue or pivot path, the cheapest test to run, and a short premortem

Never grade your own homework: the lenses stay separate so agreement means something. In Claude Code it can run each lens as its own subagent; in the plain Claude app it runs them as separate passes, and you can paste any lens into a fresh chat for full independence. Analysis only: it never contacts, posts or buys anything.

**Before:** "Great idea! Here are 10 tips to get started."
**After:** "Skip as pitched: buyers won't pay at this price, and three lenses found that on their own. The narrower version for one niche survives. Cheapest test: a pre-order page shown to 20 people in that niche this week."

**Credits:** a Nuggts original. The premortem step is Gary Klein's method.
